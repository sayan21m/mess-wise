Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Overview

MessWise ("we", "our", "the app") is developed by SR Tech. This policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and the choices you have when using the MessWise Android application and website.

Information we collect

  • Account data: Name, email address, and mess membership details when you register or join a mess.
  • Mess data: Meal attendance, expenses, cash-in records, dues, and admin settings stored in Firebase Realtime Database for your mess group.
  • Device data: Basic app version and crash reports via Firebase Crashlytics to improve stability.

How we use your information

We use collected data solely to operate mess management features — tracking meals, calculating dues, generating reports, and syncing data between mess members. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Data storage & security

Data is stored on Google Firebase infrastructure. Sensitive preferences on your device are encrypted using Android EncryptedSharedPreferences. Financial screens use anti-screenshot protection where enabled.

Data sharing

Your mess data is visible to other members and admins within the same mess. We do not share data outside your mess except as required by law or to provide core Firebase hosting services.

Your rights

You may request correction or deletion of your account data by contacting us. Mess admins may remove member records from their mess.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email srtechfly85@gmail.com or use our contact form.
