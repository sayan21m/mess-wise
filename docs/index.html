Features

Everything your mess needs

Simple tools for members and managers — synced in real time.

Real-time tracking

Meal attendance, pending dues up to today, and balances update instantly for every member.

Expense & cash-in

Log groceries and contributions with this-month and all-time totals in one place.

Month-end settlement

See who you should pay (or who pays you). Optional UPI — saved only on your phone, never in the cloud.

How it works

Up and running in minutes

  1. Create Mess

    Set up your mess with a name and admin account.

  2. Invite Members

    Share your Mess ID — members join in seconds.

  3. Book Meals

    Mark attendance, apply leave, and see today's menu.

  4. Track Expenses

    Record purchases and member cash-in by category.

  5. Monthly Settlement

    Clear last month’s dues with matched payees — pay via UPI if you want, then confirm in-app.

After a month ends, MessWise matches members who still owe with members who have surplus. You only see your own payees or expected receipts. Settlement is for the previous month — the current month stays in progress on Home as “up to today.”
